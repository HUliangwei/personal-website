## Overview

This project turns embodied-AI learning into reproducible engineering through the public [HUliangwei/robot](https://github.com/HUliangwei/robot) workspace: it fully reproduces the LeRobot PushT task in a self-built MuJoCo environment (dataset → ACT training → dual-environment inference) and uses the same workspace to move toward 3D manipulation (LIBERO) and VLA models.

## Problem / Motivation

PushT is a classic task for verifying an imitation-learning loop. The goal is to split "dataset, policy training, inference, and evaluation" into verifiable stages and achieve coverage comparable to the official pymunk environment in a self-built MuJoCo environment, instead of staying at the level of tool names.

## My Role

Learner and reproduction engineer: setting up the workspace, training and inference scripts, and a self-built MuJoCo environment; aligning physics parameters, recording results, and documenting the process in notes and docs.

## Architecture

The workspace is organized into sub-projects:

- **embodied_learning (PushT)** — main line complete. From LeRobot dataset, through ACT training, to closed-loop inference in both the official pymunk 2D and the self-built MuJoCo environments.
- **libero (LIBERO benchmark)** — scoped. Franka + MuJoCo tabletop manipulation along an ACT → SmolVLA learning route.

## Design / Method

Evidence comes from reproducible artifacts: every stage ships environment scripts, training configs, rollout outputs, and metric files; official-environment results are recorded separately from self-built-environment results, with physics differences investigated independently.

## Implementation

- A self-built MuJoCo PushT environment aligned with the official pymunk environment (joint damping and friction), including a fix for the "block does not stop immediately" physics-fidelity issue.
- ACT inference supports official checkpoints (aadarshram / Lemon-03) and a self-trained checkpoint (25k steps).
- A local web dashboard (`gui/server.py`) for progress, commands, videos, and file browsing.

## Verification / Experiments

- Official environment reproduced: aadarshram ACT @seed1000 coverage **0.9534** (finished in 134 steps).
- Self-trained ACT (25k steps) coverage 0.39 / 0.82.
- After the physics-fidelity fix, total MuJoCo rollout reward increased by **+104%**.

## Results

The PushT main line is complete and public in the repository; a known residual issue (the block still rotates more in real MuJoCo rollouts than in the official environment, 380° vs 56°) is recorded in PROGRESS.md. LIBERO and VLA remain learning topics, not completed tools.

## Challenges & Decisions

The main challenge is physics fidelity: contact-model differences between MuJoCo and pymunk cause inconsistent block motion. The fix aligns damping and friction item by item and quantifies differences with reward and rotation metrics, recording "fixed" and "residual" issues separately.

## What I Learned

A shift from "learning tool names" to "reproducing a verifiable closed loop": every stage of data, training, inference, and evaluation needs artifacts behind it, and simulation-physics consistency affects policy evaluation conclusions more than expected.
