## Overview

This project comes from a Baidu internship from 2023.09 to 2023.12. Its scope was Ansys HFSS 3D electromagnetic simulation of circuit / microwave structures related to superconducting quantum chips. The published work is **simulation only** and does not extend to any physical-device experiment claim.

## Problem / Motivation

Microwave response and field distribution depend jointly on geometry and electromagnetic setup. The project used a controlled simulation workflow to study geometric changes and inform structure analysis and optimization.

## Architecture

The simulation workflow was: 3D geometry → materials → boundary conditions → ports → mesh → sweep → field / response analysis → geometry optimization.

## Design / Method

Materials, boundaries, ports, and mesh were configured in HFSS. Parameter sweeps compared geometry changes, while electromagnetic fields and geometric relationships were used to interpret simulation trends. This conceptual workflow is not presented as measurement hardware or an actual chip layout.
