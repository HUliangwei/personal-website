## Overview

This project develops a 1×16-channel SPAD readout IC in SMIC 180 nm BCD. Its publication-safe scope spans the analog front end, discrimination, quench and reset, digital control, high-/low-voltage interfaces, I/O and ESD, and FPGA integration. It is at the **post-layout simulation / pre-tapeout** stage; this must not be read as completed tapeout or silicon measurement.

## Problem / Motivation

A SPAD readout must turn a transient single-photon event into a signal that can be discriminated, reset, and consumed by a digital system. The engineering problem connects 1.8 V logic, 5 V circuitry, and interfaces associated with an external approximately 60 V bias while keeping their boundaries explicit and verifiable.

## Architecture

The disclosed per-channel chain is: SPAD interface → analog front end (AFE) → discriminator → quench / reset → digital readout and control. The IC also includes high-/low-voltage interfaces and I/O / ESD, with FPGA-based board control and data-path integration.

## Design / Method

- Voltage domains: 1.8 V, 5 V, and interfaces associated with an external approximately 60 V bias.
- Channel organization: 1×16. The source resume uses “pixel” and “channel” inconsistently for a board-power statement, so this page retains the “pixel / channel” caveat and labels the measurement level.
- Design tools: Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, and Calibre.
- Physical implementation: floorplan and power-network work with DRC, LVS, and PEX flows.

Implementation covers the AFE, discriminator, quench, reset, digital logic, high-/low-voltage interfaces, I/O / ESD, and FPGA interface. No PDK, process rules, GDS, complete netlist, proprietary dimensions, or unreleased layout is published.

## Verification / Experiments

The verification flow includes circuit simulation in Virtuoso / Spectre, Calibre DRC / LVS / PEX, and board-level FPGA integration. Board measurement, pre-layout simulation, and post-layout status are treated as different evidence types rather than interchangeable results.

## Results

- **Measured (board-level):** the resume records board-level per-pixel / channel average power decreasing from approximately 1 W to approximately 0.1 W. Because the source mixes “pixel” and “channel,” both terms are retained; this measured result is not presented as simulated per-channel ASIC power.
- **Pre-layout simulation:** approximately 2 mW static power per IC channel and approximately 200 mW peak power during the quench transient.
- **Post-layout status:** post-layout simulation and pre-tapeout work are in progress; no verified post-layout numeric result is published.

## Challenges & Decisions

The central challenge is coordinating fast transients, multiple voltage domains, replicated channels, power integrity, and verifiable interfaces. The publication decision is to preserve evidence boundaries instead of collapsing board optimization, pre-layout simulation, and post-layout progress into one performance claim.
