## Overview

Videoto3D is a local-first video-to-3D reconstruction Studio: feed it a video, and it performs subject segmentation, camera recovery, and dense reconstruction entirely on your machine, producing Mesh (GLB) or Gaussian Splat (PLY) 3D assets.

## Features

- **Input preparation**: FFmpeg frame extraction plus SAM2 subject segmentation turns a video into a clean, reconstruction-ready image sequence.
- **Camera recovery**: COLMAP recovers sparse geometry and camera poses.
- **Dual reconstruction routes**:
  - **OpenMVS route**: dense reconstruction produces a Mesh, exportable as GLB.
  - **Brush route**: Gaussian Splat training produces PLY.
- **Local-first**: the whole pipeline runs locally; no data is uploaded to third-party services.

## Architecture

- **Language / ecosystem**: Python with FFmpeg, SAM2, COLMAP, OpenMVS, Brush, and Blender.
- **Viewer**: `gui/viewer` renders Gaussian-splat PLY with the same Spark path used on this site's Home page.
- **Pipeline**: frame extraction → segmentation → sparse reconstruction → dense reconstruction / splat training → export / preview.

## Key Implementation

- **Segmentation first**: SAM2 separates the foreground subject before COLMAP and downstream reconstruction, reducing background interference.
- Both routes share the same input preparation and camera recovery results, diverging only at the final reconstruction stage.
- The PLY viewer and the portfolio Home share the `SparkRenderer + SplatMesh` rendering path from `@sparkjsdev/spark`.

## Challenges & Decisions

Camera shake, occlusion, and background noise significantly affect reconstruction quality; the segmentation pipeline reduces this interference, while the local-first nature requires pinned tool versions and reproducible configuration.

## Results & Next Steps

An end-to-end pipeline from video to Mesh / GLB and Gaussian Splat / PLY is complete. Next steps include robustness testing across more shooting scenarios, automated reconstruction parameters, and turning successful cases into reproducible example scripts.
